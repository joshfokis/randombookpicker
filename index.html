Random Book Picker

Goodreads retired their RSS feeds (Oct 2026), so the app reads the official library export: on goodreads.com (logged in as you) go to My Books → Import / Export → "Export your library", then pick the downloaded file here. It is read entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere, and no account access is involved. Covers come from OpenLibrary and descriptions from Google Books — no keys, no CORS proxies, no Goodreads network calls at all.
sample_library.csv is missing from this deployment — commit it next to index.html to bring back the demo button.
